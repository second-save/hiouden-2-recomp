Hiouden II - English (native recomp)
====================================

This is Hiouden II (Wolf Team, Taiwanese DOS release by Softstar, 1995) rebuilt to run natively on macOS, Windows
and Linux, with an English translation. It contains NO game files: you need your own copy of the Taiwanese DOS
release (the folder with HIO.EXE, HIOSYS.EXE, A.CHA ...).

How to play
1. Start "Hiouden II" (macOS: Hiouden II.app; Windows: HioudenII.exe; Linux: ./hiouden2).
   macOS: the app is not notarized. The first time, right-click it and choose Open (or allow it in
   System Settings > Privacy & Security).
2. Click "Choose game folder..." and pick the folder of your Hiouden II install.
   The launcher copies the game into its own folder and applies the English patch there. Your folder is
   never changed. A different version, or a copy that is already patched, is refused.
3. Click Play.

Controls: mouse (left and right buttons) and keyboard, as in the original. Alt+Enter toggles fullscreen.
Esc asks whether to quit (Enter quits, Esc goes back; the game is paused meanwhile). Save first with the Quill.

Playing the game
- Right-click anything on screen for a help box saying what it does. That's the quickest way to learn.
- Mark and Mirlei have to fight their way out, floor by floor. The windows show the floor map, the battle view
  and your characters. The desk at the bottom right holds the game's controls:
    Hourglass (blue)  pauses or resumes time. Time starts paused: click it and your unit starts marching.
                      Click it again to pause, look around and change settings.
    Candlestick       game speed (3 levels)
    Quill             save            Record Book   load
    Red Orb           open another unit window    Retry   restart this stage
    Big-Mouth Fish    drag unwanted characters or items to it to get rid of them
- Fighting is automatic: when your unit meets monsters, your characters attack on their own. The unit window's
  icons set how it acts: Action Setup (behaviour), Battle Setup (tactics), Magic Setup (which spells it casts),
  Direction Setup (where it marches), Attack Mode (magic or weapons). Click a portrait to see that character's
  items.
- Save often with the Quill. Esc asks whether to quit.

Where things go
- macOS:   ~/Library/Application Support/Second Save/Hiouden II/
- Windows: %APPDATA%\Second Save\Hiouden II\
- Linux:   ~/.local/share/Second Save/Hiouden II/
  game/ holds the patched copy and your saves; launcher.cfg holds the launcher settings.
- No dialog on your Linux desktop? Install from a terminal: ./hiouden2 --install /path/to/game

Known issues
- The status popup's weakness element uses 2-letter names (Fi = fire, Ic = ice, Th = thunder, St = star).
- Saves made with the Chinese game keep their original unit names: start a new game.
