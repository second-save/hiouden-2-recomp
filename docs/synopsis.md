# Hiouden II - story synopsis for translators

Built from the Chinese script (`script/OP_E.json`, `VIS.json`, `TM.json`, `ED_E.json`, field `zh`). Names follow
`glossary.md`; anything marked (provisional) is not in the glossary yet: propose it in your report, don't invent
another spelling. Sections marked **LATE REVEAL** must not leak into earlier text (no hints in item names, early
dialogue or floor messages).

## Premise
Sequel to Hiouden (the "Scarlet King" story). Richard A. MacIntyre, third heir of the house of MacIntyre, bound
demons by pact, drove off the horse nomads and united the continent, but was feared and shunned as the Scarlet
King. In old age he left with the dryad Beatrix for the spirit world of the trees. Six years before the game, on
his way out, he met a frightened little girl in a small wood, gave her Beatrix's ring "for her future" and left
with a sad backward look. Without Richard the kingdom rotted: a witch hunt began, and the girl, marked by the odd
power the ring gave her, was jailed to await execution. The whole game is a jailbreak: the Dungeon is sealed, its
stair to the surface has been destroyed, and the only way out is down, floor by floor (21 stages), to the
teleport circles and the man behind it all.

## Main cast
- **Markfiend (Mark)** - prisoner in the next cell, a sorcerer who knows summoning magic. Starts the game asking
  to be executed; frees Mirlei mostly out of irritation. Curt, sarcastic, rough ("we" as 咱們), hides kindness
  behind insults. Mirlei and his brother call him Mark.
- **Mirlei** - the girl from the opening, now a teenager jailed as a witch; healer, wears the spirit ring.
  Polite, frightened at first, pleading, but stubborn and steady; asks gentle questions.
- **Jadain** - warden of the Dungeon (地牢總管), illusionist, Lezard's man. Smug, mocking chuckle (咈咈), calls
  his own soldiers idiots and treats soldiers and demons as pawns. Hides rooms and items with illusions.
- **Lezard Valeth** - court sorcerer, final villain. Runs the kingdom through the king and is preparing to summon
  an evil god under the Dungeon. Silky, amused, drawn-out mockery; sees himself as building a paradise.
- **The talking statue** - a crumbling statue holding demons that someone summoned from the Demon Realm and
  sealed when they refused to serve him. Asks Markfiend to free them; no pact needed. Formal, ancient voice.
- **The king's brother (王兄)** - appears only in the ending (see below). Name not given.
- **Richard / Beatrix** - opening only (and as phantoms on a late floor; see stage 18 below).
- **Guards, soldiers, prisoners** - crude jailers; a gallery of prisoners and odd characters on the floors.

## Plot in order
**Opening (OP_E).** Narration of Richard's legend, the meeting in the wood, the gift of the ring, the six years of
decay and witch hunts, ending on the girl in a cell "awaiting death".

**Scene 0 - prison intro (VIS).** Footsteps. Markfiend, in his cell, hopes it is finally his turn to die. Mirlei
begs the guard to release her for her parents' sake. The guard mocks her: confess you cursed the king with black
magic and you'll die quicker. When he goes to drag her out, something happens to him (he screams; the source
implies Mirlei's power or Markfiend's magic, it is not said). Markfiend: "Move!" He plans to get her to the
surface and then quietly go back to dying.

**Scene 1 - the talking statue.** At the top of a stair a ruined statue speaks: its demons were forced out of the
Demon Realm and sealed for refusing to serve "a man of great power". Free them and they will come when called,
no pact. It sneers that Markfiend is too green to command demons, and tells him to answer with deeds.

**Scene 2 - soldier reports to Jadain.** Two escapees are heading for the surface. Jadain already has a plan and
warns his men off: the demon he released from B2 won't care whose side they are on.

**Scene 3 - the broken stair.** The stair to the surface is split in two by "overwhelming force". Jadain: a
dungeon like this never needed an exit; he wrecked it himself because Lord Lezard ordered that neither of them
leave alive. But the teleport circles the staff use lie below. Markfiend asks about the soldiers and prisoners;
Jadain: everyone brought here dies, soldiers and demons are pawns. He will wait "in the depths". Mirlei: if there
is a way out, she will follow him.

**Scene 4 - the ring.** A life-size statue of a woman glows; Mirlei's ring burns her finger. Markfiend takes it,
mocks it as a tacky ordinary ring, then chants a rhyming spell: script-like marks and white light appear. The ring
does not protect anyone: it is a medium for summoning spirits. He is delighted he won't have to summon "those
guys" himself. (From here Mirlei summons spirits with it.)

**Scene 5 - Jadain's phantoms.** "Dance with the phantoms I made until you die."

**Scene 6 - sea of fire.** A magical barrier of flame. Only a high spirit can get them through: Markfiend hands
the job to Mirlei.

**Scene 7 - the healing talk.** Quiet rest scene. Markfiend asks when her healing power began. She doesn't know;
people treated her differently but kindly; what saddens her is that she can never live an ordinary life. He cuts
her off, apologises, tells her to cheer up. Keep it soft; it is the only tender scene.

**Scene 8 - Lezard Valeth.** Markfiend accuses Lezard of leaving the rotten government to the king while he
prepares to summon an evil god. Lezard teases him: wasn't he the one begging to be killed? Markfiend: he changed
his mind, he will kill Lezard and then leave; he used Lezard's own teleport circles, leaving the idiot underlings
stuck at the Dungeon entrance. Lezard: come and try; he will wait further in.

## Floors (TM messages; TMnn is a message file, only roughly a floor)
- **TM01-TM04 (upper cells, B1-B3 area):** prisoners explain the sewers around the Dungeon, fickle monsters, the
  "Ten Sages' Gems" (collect 10 red gems), a madman raving about crosses. Mary reports the Scarlet King's sword
  sealed deep below, where "a nostalgic melody" plays. Alarm; a grave robber (Ike) has claimed all the treasure.
- **TM05-TM10:** system messages: walls shifting by compass direction, gates and doors opened by keys, "The
  witch who brings calamity is confined here", Jadain's posted order splitting the party into three units.
- **TM11-TM13:** riddles (candle, greed, "lifelike statues"); a lady says prisoners vanish to be executed; a
  strong woman warns the whole Dungeon is out hunting them with monsters; Skull Key and the skeleton statue's eye;
  rumours that Jadain hides rooms with illusions. Jadain appears: their luck has run out.
- **TM14-TM18 (underground lake):** the water spirit's necklace stolen by the Phantom Ice Demon; a shifty mage
  wants that necklace for himself (half-cost magic); either side rewards you (sword in a pillar / a gift). Bridges
  rise; a blue ram statue moans.
- **TM19-TM21:** the "Simple Demon Museum": deadpan bestiary plaques (slimes, a bird that denies being a bird, a
  puppet, the chimera). Inscriptions about the dead and pale ghosts.
- **TM22:** an old man: every monster on this floor is a phantom, brute force won't work; he vanishes like smoke.
  Jadain decides to finish them himself. "The Wolf's Den" and "Tea time" signs (developer jokes).
- **TM23-TM26 (fire floors):** a half-erased machine plaque (poison needles), fire riddles, "Infinite Hell",
  "pass through the gate of the Demon Realm" (paired doors are teleporters).
- **TM27-TM29:** Medusa's curse lifted; the puppeteer and his puppet lovers, the Puppet Man (木偶人) and Sherill (雪莉兒)
  (both provisional), attack; freed captives thank you. The sealed **King's Sword** (provisional): once a hero's sword, renamed and
  reshaped "in the Scarlet King's name"; whoever bears that name and all the magic stones may claim it.
- **TM30-TM32:** MKG, the planner, breaks the fourth wall: a stage-1 cheat (at "Restart", hold Ctrl and the right
  mouse button until the game starts: all monsters join you), staff introductions, "one third of the game left".
- Unit lists (`MP*.json`, not translated here) show **stage 18** pits them against "King Richard?" and similar
  question-marked phantoms, and stage 21 holds Lezard and the "King of Demon Spirits" (魔精之王, provisional).

## Ending (ED_E)
Lezard, beaten, curses Markfiend for ruining his paradise in this "hell of lost souls". Markfiend: Lezard is the
one who made it hell. Markfiend sends Mirlei outside. **LATE REVEAL:** the king's brother (王兄) begs "Mark, for
our brotherhood, spare me"; Markfiend says even if Lezard fooled him, it doesn't undo the people he killed, and
does not spare him. So Markfiend is of the royal family (see Ambiguities). Outside, he points Mirlei to her
village to the east and tells her to hide for a while. She begs him to throw his weapon away: they are free, they
thought they would die here. "Die? Don't be stupid." He turns to go; she cries "Don't go!" In his backward glance
she sees Richard's face from six years ago and understands what the old king felt; the farewell is repeating
itself. Then the staff roll (Japanese staff, then the Taiwanese port team; publisher 大宇資訊 = Softstar).

## Recurring places and items
- the Dungeon (地牢/地下牢) and its floors; the stair to the surface; teleport circles (傳送用魔法陣).
- Beatrix's ring / the spirit ring: carved with a spirit seal; a summoning medium, not a charm. Central link
  between Richard, Mirlei and the ending.
- the talking statue(s) and sealed demons; the Demon Realm.
- keys: Cell Key, Silver Key, Ruby Key, Dragon Key, Serpent Key, Skull Key (provisional English for all).
- Ten Sages' Gems, magic stones (魔石), the King's Sword (王者之劍), the Spirit Necklace (all provisional).

## Ambiguities (flag, don't resolve silently)
- 王兄: literally "royal elder brother"; with 看在兄弟一場 he is Markfiend's brother, probably the reigning king
  (Lezard "left the government to the king"). Whether Markfiend is a prince is never said outright. Render as "my
  brother" / "the king's brother" until the user rules.
- Who hurts the guard in scene 0 (Mirlei's power or Markfiend's magic) is left implicit; keep it implicit.
- Why Markfiend was jailed and why he wanted to die are never explained.
- TM30 writes 緋王傳Ⅱ' (with a prime): probably the hard-disk edition; ask before rendering "Hiouden II'".
- "Ten Sages' Gems" vs "red lotus gems" (紅蓮寶石): same item, two names in one line.
